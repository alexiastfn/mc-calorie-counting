Append-only log of AI use on this project, one section per team member (handout, Section 6).
Each entry: (1) what I was trying to do, (2) tool + prompt (verbatim), (3) what the tool produced,
(4) what I accepted / rejected / changed and why (my answers verbatim), (5) how I verified it.

Conventions: text in quotes or code blocks marked "verbatim" is exactly what I typed. Text marked
"Summary (tool-written)" is the AI tool's own summary of its output, not my words. Code produced by
the tool is labelled as generated by the tool.
